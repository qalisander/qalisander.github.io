  1. OpenZeppelin 2024 – present

    First smart-contract library for Arbitrum Stylus

    Shipped the first open-source contracts library for the Stylus VM, an EVM-compatible WASM runtime. It covers ERC-20, ERC-721, ERC-1155, proxies and more. The library has passed three security audits, and I had a major hand in shaping the first test framework for Stylus.

  2. OpenZeppelin 2024 – 2025

    On-chain cryptography library

    Led development of a gas-efficient cryptography module: big integers, Montgomery-form finite fields, the ZK-friendly Poseidon hash, the Pedersen hash and Ed25519 signatures. Poseidon on Stylus runs 18× cheaper in gas than the Solidity equivalent.

    • Rust
    • Finite fields
    • Poseidon
    • EdDSA
  3. Gridlock 2023 – 2024

    MPC threshold signing for a decentralised wallet

    Built a production Rust library for multi-party key generation, transaction signing and key recovery, based on the Gennaro–Goldfeder 2020 protocol. Added 2FA secret recovery with Shamir's secret sharing, and set up CI that runs integration tests and MPC protocol-compatibility checks.

    • Rust
    • MPC
    • ECDSA
    • EdDSA
    • NATS

How engagements work

I contract directly with clients as an independent business. These are the default terms, and each services agreement can adjust them.

Scope & pricing
Fixed-scope statements of work or time-and-materials retainers. Milestones, deliverables and acceptance criteria are agreed in writing before work starts.
Invoicing
Monthly invoices against delivered milestones or logged hours, paid by bank transfer. Currency and payment terms are set in the agreement.
Working model
Fully remote and asynchronous by default, with regular written status updates. I use my own equipment, software and infrastructure.
Who does the work
I do all the work personally. No subcontracting without the client's written agreement.
Confidentiality & IP
NDAs and IP assignment are available on request. Open-source deliverables are published under the licence the client chooses.
Response time
Initial reply within one business day. A written proposal follows within three business days of the intro call.
